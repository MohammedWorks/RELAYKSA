Questions

Who can post requests on Relay?

Any registered company or government entity in Saudi Arabia — corporates, venues, agencies, ministries, authorities and municipalities.

Who can become a supplier?

Licensed event management companies, staffing and manpower companies, equipment and logistics firms, and freelancers with a valid freelance permit.

How fast will I get quotes?

For short-term events we aim to send matched quotes within 48 hours. Long-term contracts usually take one to two weeks to finalise.

How does Relay make money?

Organizers post requests for free. We charge suppliers a small fee on contracts they win through Relay.

Have an event or contract coming up?

Tell us what you need. We'll come back with matched suppliers and quotes.
