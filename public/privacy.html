Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Draft

What we collect

When you post a request or apply as a supplier, we store the details you enter: organization, contact name, work email, mobile number and the information about your request or services.

How we use it

To match requests with suitable suppliers, to contact you about your request or application, and to verify suppliers. We only share a request with suppliers we invite to quote. We don't sell your data.

Where it's stored

On Cloudflare's infrastructure. We keep it for as long as your account or request is active, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

In line with Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), you can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing privacy@relayksa.com.
